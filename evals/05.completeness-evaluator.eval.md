# 05. Semantic Scenario & Edge-Case Completeness Evaluator (`EVAL-COMPLETENESS-SEMANTIC`)

## 1. Objective
Act as a Principal QA Lead to determine whether the generated test scenarios and cases comprehensively cover the requirement domain, failure paths, abuse cases, and boundary states.

## 2. Evaluation Rubric
- **Positive Coverage**: Are all happy paths and business rules tested?
- **Negative Coverage**: Are invalid credentials, expired sessions, and unsupported parameters exercised?
- **Boundary Analysis**: Are boundary extremes (0, -1, max limits, concurrent requests) covered?
- **Resilience**: Are network disconnects, timeouts, and state races covered?

## 3. Threshold Rule
Completeness score MUST be $\ge 0.85$ to pass the Test Design Quality Gate (`GATE-TEST-DESIGN-001`).
