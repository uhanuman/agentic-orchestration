# 07. Enterprise Quality Gate Hierarchy & Waiver Policy

## 1. Quality Gate Hierarchy

### Critical Gates (Zero-Bypass Policy)
- **GATE-SCHEMA-001**: Domain Schema Integrity (`EVAL-SCHEMA-001`).
- **GATE-REF-001**: Upstream Traceability & Referential Invariants (`EVAL-REFERENTIAL-001` / `EVAL-TRACEABILITY-001`).
- **GATE-VAL-001**: Hallucination Drift $\le 0.10$ (`EVAL-DRIFT-SEMANTIC`).
*Zero waiver bypass is permitted under any circumstances for Critical Gates.*

### Major Gates (Waiver Governed)
- **GATE-REQ-001**: Requirement Ambiguity $\le 0.15$ (`EVAL-AMBIGUITY-SEMANTIC`).
- **GATE-TEST-DESIGN-001**: Completeness $\ge 0.85$ & Coverage $\ge 0.85$ (`EVAL-TEST-COVERAGE-001`).
- **GATE-SDET-001**: P0/P1 Automation Feasibility $\ge 1.0$ (`EVAL-TEST-COVERAGE-001`).
*Can be bypassed only via a cryptographically signed Human Waiver Token.*

### Minor Gates (Informational)
- **GATE-A11Y-001**: Accessibility WCAG 2.1 AA recommendations.
- **GATE-PERF-001**: Latency SLA warnings under non-peak load.
